ESSAY 01 • MARKET ENTRY

By Soumya Ranjan Behera

Why Tier-2 university hubs are India's highest-leverage builder secret.

Most foreign teams land in Bengaluru, host one expensive dinner, and wonder why developer retention drops off two weeks later. While Bengaluru remains indispensable for senior operators and partnerships, Tier-2 engineering hubs offer hungry, deeply committed student and early-career builders who stay loyal to the ecosystems that invest in teaching them first.

When we onboarded 10+ universities and launched dedicated campus clubs during Arbitrum Launchpad India, the highest workshop-to-submission conversion came from campuses where we paired hands-on buildstations with ongoing local club leadership.

ESSAY 02 • PARTNERSHIPS

By Soumya Ranjan Behera

Qualification over volume: How to structure B2B and ecosystem partnerships in India.

A logo swap announcement on X is not a partnership. Real distribution partnerships in India—whether with dev agencies, system integrators, regional communities or complementary SaaS tools—only move the needle when both sides agree on a concrete activation plan within the first 30 days.

Before signing any partner MOU, we qualify four things: audience overlap, technical readiness, co-marketing or referral bandwidth, and a named point-of-contact who owns the relationship week to week.
